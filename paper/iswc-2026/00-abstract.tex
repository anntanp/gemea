GEMEA is the first open knowledge graph over the German Digital Library (DDB) corpus, making 26.8 million digitized objects queryable via SPARQL, browsable as Linked Data, and accessible to AI agents through a Model Context Protocol (MCP) interface.
The MOCHO alignment layer maps DDB-EDM metadata to RDA and domain-specific ontologies through corpus-driven dispatch, with GND authority linking for literary and musical works.
GEMEA supports research on KG construction, ontology evaluation, and LLM-grounded cultural heritage retrieval.
